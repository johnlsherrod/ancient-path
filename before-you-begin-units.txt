BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Here I Am

In the opening pages of the Bible, God's first question to a hiding Adam wasn't an accusation — it was Where are you? Known by Jesus is one path into answering that question — not the whole of it. The mirror, the slower walk, the table: each one worked on the same hiding Adam knew, and shaped how you'd answer. Here I Am is where you finally give your answer — in your own words, to God and to the men around you.

Twenty-seven lines, five parts. Each one starts for you — you finish it in your own words.

=== Do Hard Things · section 18 · unit "Before You Begin" (ebook6aa75a0b55c21bdfcc0b93fd-6aa75a0b5fe58) ===
BEFORE YOU BEGIN
The Man Who Crossed

Do Hard Things was one path into the work you're doing — not the whole of it. Before Peter walked on water, there was another storm: the disciples afraid, Jesus asleep, until they woke Him and He rebuked the wind and the waves. That storm taught them who He was — and it's what let Peter, in the next one, hear "Come" and actually step out of the boat. Twelve weeks worked the same way: not one act of strength, but lesson after lesson, each one Christ used to prepare you for what came next. This is where you name what that preparation built, and what the last hard thing — the crossing — finally cost.

Twenty-five lines, five parts. Each one starts for you — you finish it in your own words.
