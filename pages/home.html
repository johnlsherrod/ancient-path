Story → Refining → Testimony.

Our foundational lens is narrative-focused trauma care from the Allender Center. We begin with the story a man is actually living, let it be tested and refined, and stay until it becomes testimony — something he can stand on and speak. This same movement runs through every path and every form.

Walked, not managed. This work is held by trained men — NFTC certification, confessional-community formation, and a vetting standard for everyone who leads — and by experience: we have walked hundreds of men through the Breaking Free process over the past ten years. What you bring here is held in confidence. Our standards.
