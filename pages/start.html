Why do this at all

A story you have never told still runs your life. It shows up as the promises you made to yourself as a boy — never again, never trust, never need anyone — and as the walls you built to stay safe, and it decides how you react before you have had time to think. Telling it changes that. Writing it down, in the past tense, is a first step toward being known — and that is where healing happens, in company, not alone. Told out loud and heard, it gets new information: you are not alone with it now, and you are safe. That is what new ways of living are built on. And a story told truly, all the way through, becomes a testimony: something you can hand to another man when he needs it.
